# "You're losing them. Here's the fix." problem → solution reel (Sun 22 Nov)
File: `lazytradie-problem-solution-reel-9x16.mp4` (62.6 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, stereo, -14 LUFS, 70 MB). Cover: `cover-9x16.png` ("money in the bin", safe for the 4:5 grid crop).

How it's built (v2, the owner's notes from 22 Nov applied):
- The owner's 20 VO lines, all kept in full, in order. Built in `src/` (`prep.py` → `node render.js` → `encode.sh`; `node cover.js` for the cover).
- The owner's chosen track (`atlasaudio-sci-fi-ambient`) is cut so the beat grid (115 BPM) drives the edit. Every scene lasts a whole number of beats, and the drum drop lands on "Meet LazyTradie". It's ducked under the voice.
- **Problem half:** pure jet black (no navy), lit by magenta, cyan and amber glows that drift scene to scene. The UI follows the owner's reference reel: white UI cards, a black voice pill with a live waveform, mono labels that type on, words rising out of a mask, an orbit clock for "two hours later", a serif quote, a hand-drawn marker circle, a pull-back from full-bleed into a card, a zoom-through, and a text cloud.
- **The drop:** an iris opens from a spark onto the reference's blush world. The orb and mono labels lead into the wordmark lockup, then a slot carousel (Calls → Texts → Web chats) on a sky. The camera tilts down to a sunset with the calendar window, then an approval card with an annotation arrow and a tap, the call log and transcript with marker circles, a fanned deck of the campaign photos, and the end lockup.
- The owner's clips play at full quality, cropped so the Gemini ✦ watermark is cut out of every shot.
- The sound is rebuilt (`src/sfx.py`): a real phone buzz, Australian ringback and line drop, keys, a marker squeak, a slot-machine ratchet, bin thunks, coin clinks, a lid slam, clock ticks, glass bells and a sub boom on the drop. Each sound is placed on its animation event, with a short stereo room, about 8 dB under the voice.
- **No price anywhere.** The end card says: $0 setup fee · Live in minutes · Comment "DEMO" · lazytradie.com.au.
