## 3. Putting our own voice on Veo clips (and keeping lip sync)

Veo writes its own voice into every clip. That's what we want for the **characters** (each one
should sound like a different person). But the **presenter** has to be our narrator, because the
same voice carries straight on over the graphics. Three ways to do it, best first:

**A. Veo + ElevenLabs Voice Changer (recommended for the presenter).**
1. Generate the clip in Veo with the exact line written in the prompt, so the mouth moves to the
   right words.
2. In ElevenLabs open **Voice Changer** and upload the Veo MP4, or just its audio. Pick the
   narrator voice, turn on **Remove background noise**, and generate. This is speech-to-speech: it
   keeps every syllable's timing, pause and emphasis and only swaps the voice, so the lips still
   match.
3. Send me the untouched Veo MP4 plus the Voice Changer file. I line them up to the sample by
   matching the two speech waveforms, put clean room tone underneath and mix it.

The catch: Voice Changer keeps the source's delivery and accent. So the prompt asks Veo for a calm,
deep, Australian male read. Regenerate if the read is wrong, because the timbre swap can't fix
delivery.

**B. ElevenLabs Chimera (the tool in this reference).** In ElevenCreative pick the Chimera
template, upload a still of the presenter (the `IMG_P` prompt), write the line, choose the voice,
and add a short motion prompt. It speaks in an ElevenLabs voice and animates the face to it, so
sync is built in. The trade-offs: output is up to 720p (the reference itself is 720p), it's
brand-new, and I couldn't confirm that a cloned voice works in it (library and designed voices
do). Reports also say it isn't available in the US. If your narrator voice is selectable there,
it's the simplest route.

**C. Lip-sync after the fact.** ElevenLabs' Image & Video section has third-party lip-sync models.
They take a clip or still plus your audio. They're useful to fix one line later without
regenerating, but less reliable on big close-ups, so they're a fallback.

**Characters:** keep Veo's own voices and ambience. If one comes out with the wrong accent or a
flat read, regenerate it, or run that clip through Voice Changer with a fitting voice.
