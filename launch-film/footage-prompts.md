**For every clip:**
- Use 16:9, the longest length available (8 s), and download at the highest resolution offered
  (1080p if available). Leave a moment of silence before and after each line so I have room to cut.
- Make 2–4 takes of each and keep the best. Name the file after its clip ID (`LF_04.mp4`) and don't
  trim or re-encode it.
- Every prompt already ends with "no music, no subtitles", because Veo sometimes adds both.
