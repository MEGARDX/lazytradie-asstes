# LazyTradie Tutorial: Job Tiers. Photo Prompts (8 images)

Same look as the explainer photos: candid, natural light, Australian, nobody looking at the
camera. Save each to `Tiers/assets/photos/` with the filename shown. Generate at 1536 px or more
on the long edge if your tool allows (1024 px works too).

**Negative prompt (use for all):**
```
text, logos, watermark, brand names, signage, cartoon, illustration, 3d render, plastic skin, oversaturated, harsh flash, looking at camera, posed stock smile, extra fingers, deformed hands
```
